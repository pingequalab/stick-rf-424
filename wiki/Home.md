## What is the Stick RF 424?

The **Stick RF 424** is a dual-band RF module for the **M5StickC Plus 1.1 and 2**. It integrates:

- **CC1101** — Sub-GHz radio (315 / 433 / 868 / 915 MHz)
- **nRF24L01+** — 2.4GHz transceiver

A **physical hardware slide switch** provides true electrical isolation between chipsets — no software conflicts, no firmware overhead. It is one of PINGEQUA's most popular products with **92+ community reviews**.
